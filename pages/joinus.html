Prospective PhD students

PhD opportunities in alloy design via defect engineering

Two positions available, starting Spring or Fall 2027. We are looking for curious and passionate PhD students to join us in understanding defects in crystalline materials and in unlocking their potential (and yours) for materials design in extreme environments, with a focus on metals and alloys.

The work: The work focuses on the fundamentals of defects in materials from theoretical and computational perspectives (atomistic simulation and CALPHAD). Specifically, we are interested in what governs defect behavior, including its thermodynamic, mechanical, and kinetic properties, and how defect properties connect to experimental observables and to materials design workflows. If you enjoy asking why nature behaves the way it does and want to put interesting physics to good use in materials applications, this could be a good fit for you.

What we are looking for: A strong academic background, with prior background in physical metallurgy and computational research, is required.

How to apply: Please email the following to nt385@njit.edu:

  • A CV
  • A transcript (unofficial or official)
  • A list of three references
  • A brief note on the research questions that interest you and what you hope to get out of a PhD (your goals)

Note: Applicants should also have IELTS or TOEFL scores and GRE scores.
